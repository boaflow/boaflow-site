SOP creation

Turn "just ask Dave" into a written SOP.

Writing the process down is one of the widest gaps on a small shop floor. Boaflow drafts the SOP or work instruction. Your people approve it. Knowledge stays in the building when someone retires, or just takes a week off.

Your expert walks the job, or hands over the notes. Boaflow does the typing. Nothing goes live on its own.

What we build

Five places the retyping hides.

The same work the shop already does. AI drafts it. A person decides what goes out.

AI doesn't replace your people. It backs them up, so they spend less time retyping and more time on the work that ships parts.

01 · Paperwork and quality

Records that follow the job

  • Inspection records drafted from the sales order, so the spec is typed once.
  • Certificates of conformance drafted for a signature and a file. The print, write, and scan loop can stop.
  • Device history records, batch records, NCRs, first-article inspections, and audit packets.
  • Lot and serial traceability, so you can say where a part has been.
02 · Parts, inventory, purchasing

From the sales order to the dock

  • The part number is set on the sales order and carried through purchasing, the traveler, QC, and shipping.
  • Live stock, lot and serial, reorder points, and a where-is-it answer.
  • Reorder POs drafted, late vendors flagged, and chase emails drafted for a person to send.
  • A promise-date board: late orders first, and every slip explained, in place of the late-order spreadsheet.
03 · Quoting and email

The inbox and the quote

  • RFQs and bid sets turned into draft quotes from past jobs and current prices.
  • Outlook sorted. Customer POs and requests turned into system records.
  • Replies drafted in your shop's voice. A person sends them.
  • Business Central kept current, so the line in Excel doesn't get typed in again by hand.
04 · People and knowledge

SOPs, the huddle, and a shop assistant

  • SOPs and work instructions drafted from a walkthrough or from notes, then approved, versioned, and tied to training. Full SOP offer.
  • A dashboard for each person, so the morning huddle is about the jobs that need a decision.
  • A shop assistant that answers from the SOPs your team has approved.
  • Parts, BOMs, orders, POs, vendors, inventory, quality records, and SOPs in one connected place.
05 · Safe AI

Your people stay in charge

  • A person approves every action. A draft stays a draft until someone on your team says go.
  • An audit trail of what was drafted and who approved it.
  • Built with ISO 13485, AS9100, and FDA quality rules in mind. Read the safe-AI section.
  • It sits on top of what you already run. The ERP, the spreadsheet, and the paper stay.
