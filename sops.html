How an SOP gets written
  1. Walk the job Your expert walks through it, or hands over their notes. The sticky note on the machine counts.
  2. Draft Boaflow writes the SOP or work instruction. Your experts teach. Boaflow does the typing.
  3. Approve Your team reviews it. Nothing goes live on its own.
  4. Train on this rev Version-controlled, and tied to training, so the next hire learns the current revision.

Before they retire
